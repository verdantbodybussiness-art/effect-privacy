Effect privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Effect (package com.effect.study) is a study planner and focus app for school students. It builds a study plan from your class, board and target score, times your focus sessions, and blocks the apps you choose while you study. This page says what Effect stores, what never leaves your phone, and how to delete your data. "We" means the team that makes Effect.

What we store on our server

When you first open Effect, it creates an anonymous account for you. There is no email address or password. These are kept with that account, so your plan and progress come back if you reinstall on the same account:

  • The first name you type, and your class, board, stream, state, target score, finish date and exam dates.
  • Your study record: focus sessions and their minutes, the chapters and topics you mark, questions solved and how many you got right, doubts, revisions and how each revised topic felt (clear, shaky or forgot), the quick check after learning a topic, your answers to the practice questions Effect asks after a practice sitting, the marks of a sample paper and the chapters where you lost marks, and the study sessions you plan (with the names you give them).
  • Where you stood when you started: the chapters you said you had done or half done, and roughly how many questions you had practised.
  • With each focus session, the chapter and topics it was on, and for a practice sitting from your NCERT book, which questions you tried (by their numbers) and which went wrong.
  • Your settings: the apps you set a daily limit on or tell Effect how to count (stored as their package names, for example com.instagram.android), and your focus and reminder settings.
  • The answers you give when you set up the app, such as which kind of app pulls your focus most.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Only if you choose to sign in with Google in Settings, so your data comes back on a new phone: your Google account's email address, name and profile picture link are kept with your Effect account by Supabase's sign-in service. Effect never sees your Google password, and it does not read anything else from your Google account. Without it, your account stays anonymous.

This is stored with Supabase, the database service Effect runs on. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the database only lets your own anonymous account read it.

What stays on your phone

  • Screen time and app usage. With Usage access, Effect reads how long you use each app to show your day, your week and the time lost to distracting apps. These numbers are worked out and kept on your phone.
  • The list of apps on your phone. Effect reads it so you can pick what to block. Only the package names of apps you set a limit on or classify are stored with your account (see above).
  • Notifications. With Notification access, Effect removes the notifications of the apps you blocked, while a focus session runs. It does not keep or send their content.
  • The share picture. When you share your study time, the picture is made on your phone and goes only where you send it.
  • Your notes from Effect. The notes on how your study is going (why a chapter is slow, the time lost to distracting apps, how many times an app was opened) are worked out on your phone from the numbers above.
  • Study apps and lecture channels. The minutes you spent in apps you marked as study during a focus session, and the YouTube channels you told Effect belong to a chapter, stay on your phone.

Location

Only if you tap "Find my state" while setting up, Effect asks for your approximate location once and turns it into the name of your state with Android's own location service. The location itself is not stored or sent to our server. The state you choose is saved with your profile. You can always pick your state from the list instead.

Permissions and why Effect asks for them

  • Usage access: to see which app is open, so a blocked app can be stopped during a focus session, and to show your screen time.
  • Display over other apps: to show the block screen over a blocked app, and session reminders.
  • Notification access (optional): to silence blocked apps' notifications while you focus.
  • Notifications: for the session timer, reminders and the revision you planned.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional): so a planned session starts on time.
  • Run in the background (optional): so the phone does not stop the blocking in the middle of a session.
  • Approximate location (optional): only for "Find my state", as described above.

Study on YouTube

Effect can show YouTube's mobile website inside the app for study videos. What you watch and any Google account you sign in with there are between you and YouTube, under YouTube's terms and Google's privacy policy. Effect does not store your watch history on our server. The channels whose videos were blocked are remembered on your phone only, so you can allow one with a tap. When you search for a channel to allow, only the words you type are sent to YouTube to find it.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics tools in the app.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with anyone, other than Supabase storing it for us.
  • We don't read the content of your messages, photos or files.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Effect removes everything it kept on your phone. To delete what is stored with your account on our server, open Effect, go to Profile, then Settings, and tap Delete my data. After you confirm, your account and everything stored with it are deleted from our server straight away, and then the copy on your phone is cleared. If you prefer, the same screen can open an email to us with your account code filled in instead; we then delete everything within 30 days and reply when it is done. You can also write to the address below; include your account code from that screen so we can find your data.

If you have already uninstalled Effect and don't have the code, write to us with the first name, class, board and state you gave Effect and roughly when you used it. We delete the account that matches and reply when it is done. If more than one account could be yours, we ask for more details before deleting anything.

Children

Effect is made for school students aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep data from them. If you think a child under 13 uses Effect, write to us and we will delete their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.

Help

If you tap "Something not working?" in Help, Effect opens an email to us holding your phone's model, your Android and Effect versions, your account code and which of Effect's permissions are on, so we can help. It is sent only if you send that email.
